The Yacht

Sailboat fun. Amenities you expect. Luxury you want.

Built in South Africa and lovingly maintained, Celestine pairs the romance of a sailing yacht with the modern amenities you expect, all in an irresistibly luxurious package.

Five Queen Cabins

Five similarly sized upscale staterooms, each with its own private ensuite head (electric toilet), private shower, sink, vanity, and dedicated under-bed storage and wardrobe.

Seamless Indoor-Outdoor Flow

An electronic window and door connect the indoor salon directly to the forward bow cockpit.

Four Social Zones

Guests can gather across a sun-drenched forward cockpit, a shaded aft deck for open-air dining, a bright panoramic salon, and a spacious flybridge with sunset views.

High-End Comforts

Equipped with full air conditioning (11 total units), a watermaker, an icemaker, a salon TV/audio entertainment system, two dishwashers, and seven refrigerators.

Onboard Private Chef

A dedicated chef prepares restaurant-quality meals tailored to your tastes, from plated dinners to casual island fare.

Water Toys

Two paddleboards, an e-foil, an underwater Seabob, an inflatable Hangout, and a tender make exploration and adventure effortless. Every anchorage becomes your playground.
